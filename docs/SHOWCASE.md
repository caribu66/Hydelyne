# Crew DMS — Showcase Guide

*By Hydenlyne.*

*Find the right offshore specialist in minutes, mobilize with confidence.*

## The problem we solve

Staffing offshore projects means digging through scattered CVs, expired
certificates, and spreadsheets — then hoping the person you pick is actually
available, qualified, and compliant on mobilization day.

Crew DMS replaces that scramble with **one trusted roster**: every
specialist, their documents, their compliance status, and their availability —
searchable in plain language and ready to mobilize.

## What the platform does

**1. Builds your roster automatically.**
Upload a CV and the platform reads it in under a second — name, experience,
training, work history — then queues it for a quick human review. Nothing
enters the searchable roster until an administrator approves it, so the data
clients see is always verified, never guessed.

**2. Finds the right specialist instantly.**
Type what you need in plain English — *"seismic observer with offshore
survival, available next month"* — and get ranked candidates with AI-written
summaries explaining why each one fits. Filter by skills, experience,
certifications, availability, or location.

**3. Compares and shortlists.**
Side-by-side candidate comparison, skill-radar views, and exportable
shortlists make it easy to present options to a client or project manager —
and to defend the choice.

**4. Keeps every mobilization compliant.**
Certifications, medicals, and document expiry are tracked continuously.
Expiring items surface before they block a mobilization, and every
mobilization request carries its compliance evidence with it.

**5. Runs the operation end to end.**
Vessel assignments, project rosters, document requests to contractors, and a
secure document vault (backed by Dropbox) keep the whole engagement in one
place — from first search to onboard.

## A consultant's journey through the platform

```text
CV uploaded → read in seconds → human review → approved to roster
  → found by search → compared & shortlisted → compliance checked
  → mobilization requested → assigned to vessel/project
```

Every step keeps its evidence: the original document, the review decision,
and the audit trail stay attached to the profile.

## Why teams trust it

- **Verified data only** — unreviewed or rejected records never appear in
  search results.
- **No guessing at intake** — CVs are read deterministically from their
  structure; anything unclear goes to human review, never to an AI guess.
- **Private by default** — role-based access, audited downloads and exports,
  and no sensitive data in logs.
- **Your documents stay yours** — secure storage with full audit history.
