# ADR NNNN: Decision title

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded | rejected
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Owners: names or team
- Supersedes: ADR number, if any

## Context

Describe the problem, constraints, current evidence, security/data impact, and
the forces that make a decision necessary.

## Decision

State the decision precisely, including ownership and dependency boundaries.

## Alternatives considered

List realistic alternatives and why they were not selected.

## Consequences

Record benefits, costs, operational burden, risks, and new constraints.

## Migration and rollback

Describe staged delivery, compatibility, data migration, verification, and how
to reverse or contain failure.

## Validation

List tests, metrics, review checkpoints, and evidence that will confirm the
decision works.
