PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY
Experienced MMO/MFO/ENV Technician with a strong maritime background in the seismic industry,
skilled in supporting offshore operations and environmental compliance. I collaborate effectively across
multidisciplinary teams to deliver high-quality environmental monitoring and ensure the protection of
marine mammals.
Passionate about marine wildlife conservation and dedicated to marine mammal observation, I combine
hands-on field expertise with a strong commitment to responsible and environmentally sustainable
offshore practices. I have participated in numerous environmental and habitat surveys, including sediment
sampling for habitat assessments and macrofaunal analysis.
Highly motivated, adaptable, and detail-oriented, I bring a proactive approach to responsibility, strong
work ethic, and continuous drive to develop my knowledge and skills within the marine environmental
sector
